What the robot sees

Pick the camera for each job. The front camera must see the table and the arm -- not the room or the people in it. The number under each picture is how many frames a second that camera is really giving.

Move the robot

The arm you hold is the leader. The other one copies it.

Start position: not set

While it is moving, hold the arm where every try should begin and press the button. After each try -- saved or thrown away -- the robot goes back there, slowly. Put the arm in your hand back there too.

Teach the robot a job

Do the job with the leader arm. The robot writes down everything it sees and everything you do. Everything stays on this computer.

One try at a time, for as long as it takes. There is no timer. Press Done when the job is finished. Press Start recording again for another try and it is added to the same job.

Watch it again

Plays a recording back on the arm by itself. Nobody holds the leader. Keep hands and faces clear before you press it.

Is an arm stuck?

If an arm will not move when you pull it, its motors are still holding on. Switching them off lets go. Hold the arm first -- it goes floppy and will drop.
